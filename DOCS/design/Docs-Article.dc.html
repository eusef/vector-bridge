Alerts and the status light

Everything here is local. The bridge never sends a notification over the internet, so alerts work off-grid and only reach a phone that has the bridge's home screen open.

What triggers an alert

An alert fires when something that was working stops. Something that has never worked is shown on the home screen but does not interrupt you.

  • Radio control lost: the radio stopped answering on CAT
  • Receive audio lost: audio from the radio stopped arriving
  • Unexpected transmit: the radio is keyed and the bridge did not ask for it

The status light

PatternMeaning
Even blink, half a second on and offNo readiness result yet, or the Wi-Fi is down
One short wink every two secondsAccess point up, waiting for a client
Steady onA client is connected
Fast flashFault: something that was working has stopped

A fault clears itself when the failed check recovers. A fault outranks everything else, so the fast flash continues even if the Wi-Fi goes down.

Hardware

Set these as Environment= lines in the tuner service. The installer does all of this for the ACT LED.

VECTOR_BRIDGE_LED=/sys/class/leds/ACT/brightness
VECTOR_BRIDGE_BEEPER=/sys/class/leds/<label>/brightness
Tip

A passive piezo needs a tone signal and is not supported by this interface.
