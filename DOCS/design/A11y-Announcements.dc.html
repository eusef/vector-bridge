Touch and keyboard

Every control is a real button, link, input or select, at least 44 by 44 px. Tab order follows reading order. Focus rings are 2 px green, or red in Night Ops.

Large text

Fonts scale with the phone. Cards reflow to one column, the two-up Station grid becomes one column at 160% and above, and no text is clipped (see the Large Text board).

Reduce Motion

Progress bars and the spinning check icon stop animating; a static icon and the text carry the state.
