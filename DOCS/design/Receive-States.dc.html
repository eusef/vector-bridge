Receive card: every state

The home page shows one of these. Each fault names its own fix; the card recovers by itself when audio is right. audio_device {ok, error, since} in the status reply tells a missing card from a quiet band.

Ready

C2No audio
Receive

No audio

The adapter is present but nothing is arriving. Transmit stays locked until receive is ready.
Silent
Try these, in order
  1. Check the cable is in the radio's headphone or data jack, not the microphone jack.
  2. Turn up the radio's volume or data output level.
  3. Unplug the USB audio adapter and plug it back in.
C3Level too low
Receive

Too quiet

Audio is arriving but below the brackets. Weak signals will be missed.
Below range
Try these, in order
  1. Turn up the radio's data output or headphone level.
  2. Then raise the capture level in Receive Audio.

Too loud

Audio is above the target range. Turn the radio's output down a little.

C4Clipping
Receive

Clipping

Audio is clipping. Decodes will fail until it is lower.
Clipping
Try these, in order
  1. Turn down the radio's data output or headphone level.
  2. Then lower the capture level in Receive Audio.
C4aFrozen
Receive

Frozen

A frozen level usually means a stuck interface. Unplug and replug the USB adapter.
Not moving
C5Adapter present, no capture input
Receive

No input

The USB audio adapter is found but has no capture channel. Usually the cable is not seated, or a combo-jack adapter has no microphone plug in it.
No channel
Try these, in order
  1. Push the audio plug fully into the adapter.
  2. On a combo jack, use a 4-pole (TRRS) plug so the adapter enables its input.
  3. Unplug the adapter and plug it back in.
C5Audio adapter missing
Receive

No adapter

No USB sound card since 3 min ago. Apps see silence and the bridge is retrying.
Try these, in order
  1. Check the USB cable between the bridge and the radio or interface.
  2. Unplug the adapter and plug it back in.
  3. With two cards plugged in, the bridge prefers the one on the radio's own USB device.
