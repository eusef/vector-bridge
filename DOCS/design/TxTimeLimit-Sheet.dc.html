Transmit Time Limit

A radio on VOX transmits for as long as audio arrives. If an app gets stuck sending audio, this limit mutes it so the radio stops.
Now 30 s.
Choosing a value
  1. 30 s, the default, fits FT8, FT4 and every JS8 speed.
  2. WSPR transmits for about 110 s, and JT65 and JT9 for about 50 s. Set 120 for those.
  3. 0 turns the limit off.

At the limit the bridge sends silence until the app pauses for 1 s. Any 1 s pause starts the count again, and digital modes pause between transmissions. Radio control keying has its own limits on the Transmit sheet.

Muting a transmission now
An app has sent 30 s of audio without a pause. The bridge is sending silence until it pauses for 1 s.
