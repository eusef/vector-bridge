D7Failed: no tone
No tone
Forward power stayed near zero. The bridge's test tone is not reaching the radio, or the radio is not in a data mode.
  1. Check the radio is in its data mode (DATA A on the KX2) with its USB or data input selected.
  2. Check the audio cable is in the radio's data or line input jack.
  3. Open the Level Tuner and watch the TX meter while the tone plays.

D8aFailed: supply sag
Stopped: supply sagged
Supply voltage dropped from 12.6 V to 10.4 V (17%) during the carrier. The bridge aborted. The battery or lead cannot carry transmit current.
  1. Charge or replace the radio's battery.
  2. Use a shorter or thicker power lead.
  3. Try again.

The radio stays at 0.1 W after this result. Set your operating power on the radio before calling.
