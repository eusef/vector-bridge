Alerts: every modal

Drawn side by side; each appears over the home page like B12. Each clears by itself when its cause goes away, and only the highest-priority one shows at a time.

E6Unexpected transmit (highest priority)

Unexpected transmit

The radio is keyed and no app owns transmit. Detected 4 s ago.

What this means
A stuck PTT line, a radio-side VOX trip, or a desktop app keying outside the bridge. The bridge tried to unkey once.
Try these, in order
  1. Tap Stop Transmitting.
  2. If the radio stays keyed, pull the keying or USB cable from the radio.
  3. Turn VOX off on the radio and the interface.

This alert stays on top of every other fault until the radio unkeys.

E5Watchdog stop

Transmit stopped

VectorComm on Phil's iPad went quiet for 3 s while transmitting. The bridge unkeyed the radio.

What this means
The app stopped sending audio, usually because Wi-Fi dropped or the app went to the background. Nothing is damaged. Transmit stays unlocked.
  1. Bring the app to the front and check its connection to the bridge.
  2. Move the phone closer to the bridge.
  3. Send again.

F1Radio control lost

Radio control lost

The radio stopped answering on its control cable. It was working until 40 s ago.

What this means
Apps cannot key the radio or read its frequency. Receive audio is not affected. Transmit is locked until control returns.
Try these, in order
  1. Check the radio is switched on.
  2. Check the control cable is in the radio's data or USB jack, not the microphone jack.
  3. Check the radio's baud rate matches the kit: 38400.

The bridge light is flashing fast. It stops, and this alert clears, when the radio answers again.

F3Transmit locked again

Transmit locked again

The bridge restarted 1 min ago, so transmit is locked until you answer the antenna question again.

Why
Transmit locks on every restart, when the radio, interface or kit changes, and when the load is changed. It never stays unlocked across a power cycle.

F2Receive audio lost

Receive audio lost

Audio from the radio stopped 2 min ago after working for 3 hours.

What this means
Apps hear silence. Transmit is locked until audio returns, since a receive fault is usually a cable fault that also breaks transmit audio.
Try these, in order
  1. Check the cable is in the radio's headphone or data jack.
  2. Turn up the radio's volume or data output level.
  3. Unplug the USB audio adapter and plug it back in.

Clears by itself when audio arrives.

E4App disconnected

App disconnected

VectorComm on Phil's iPad left 1 min ago. It was the only app streaming.

What this means
If it was transmitting, the bridge unkeyed the radio at once. Receive keeps running. Transmit stays unlocked for the next app.
  1. Check the iPad is still on this Wi-Fi.
  2. Reopen VectorComm; it rejoins by itself.

G2Battery critical

Battery critical

6% left, about 10 minutes. The bridge shuts down by itself at 3% to protect the card.

What this means
Transmit is locked now so the radio is not left keyed. Receive keeps running until shutdown.
  1. Plug the bridge into USB power.
  2. Or finish up: the bridge saves its settings before it shuts down.

A1Wi-Fi still open (transmit refused)

Wi-Fi is open

An app asked to transmit. The bridge refused because this Wi-Fi has no passphrase.

Why
Anyone nearby could join an open bridge and key your radio. Set a passphrase once; it is kept across restarts.
