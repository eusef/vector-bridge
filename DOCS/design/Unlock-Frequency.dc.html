Frequency and mode

The radio is on 10.000.000 MHz AM, outside every amateur band.

Sets the radio over radio control. Most radios will not transmit outside a ham band, for example on 10 MHz (WWV), and the bridge will not unlock there.

Outside every amateur band
The bridge will not unlock transmit on 10.000 MHz. Tune inside a ham band, or override if this frequency is allowed where you are.

What is on the antenna jack?

Asked once after each power-up. It cannot be skipped. Confirming a new load unkeys any app and resets the later steps.

Next: drive, then antenna

Drive: the bridge plays its own 1500 Hz test tone, keys the radio and steps the audio down until ALC clears. Antenna: a 2-second carrier at this radio's minimum power, 0.5 W, while reading SWR. No app is needed.

Tune inside a ham band and choose the load first.
